Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy covers the India Meridian Uploader, DCA Uploader and Trend Nazariya Uploader apps (the "apps").

Who uses the apps

The apps are private tools used only by the owner of The India Meridian, Desi Chapters Abroad and Trend Nazariya YouTube channels to manage his own channels. They are not offered to other users.

What data the apps access

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the apps use the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails, upload captions, update video details and read channel and video statistics for the owner's own channels. Access tokens are stored privately and used only for these tasks.

What the apps do not do

  • They do not collect data from other people.
  • They do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • They do not use data for advertising.

The apps' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
